About EcoAxis Global Limited

A diversified African business platform operating with global standards.

Company Overview

EcoAxis Global Limited was established to create long-term economic value through practical investments in essential sectors: property development, agriculture, and agri-food trade.

We combine local market understanding with international business practices to meet the requirements of clients, partners, and investors while maintaining responsible governance.

Vision

To be a trusted African enterprise delivering sustainable infrastructure, food systems, and trade solutions at global quality standards.

Mission

To execute high-impact projects professionally, connect value chains responsibly, and build long-term partnerships across sectors.

Core Values

Integrity, professionalism, sustainability, operational excellence, and mutually beneficial collaboration.
